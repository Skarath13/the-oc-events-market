## Design and implementation

Applied the Impeccable audit and polish guidance while retaining the existing identity, typography, navigation, hero composition, and event-video behavior. Refined mobile image candidates and crops, gallery spacing and captions, dark/light keyboard focus, 44-pixel footer touch targets, and reduced-motion behavior. Removed the delayed hero-image entrance and preloaded the existing fonts. Below-fold video fallbacks now use lazy responsive images, preserving playback and paused frames while reducing competition with the hero.

All 37 exported images have embedded EXIF, IPTC, and XMP removed. Responsive derivatives do not enlarge the source images. Unmodified originals and the three new videos are outside the public site bundle. Emoji masks are permanent image pixels, not removable HTML/CSS layers. The deterministic export script verifies source hashes; the mask manifest records each reviewed placement. The longer wedding gallery uses three desktop columns and two mobile columns with native image proportions.
